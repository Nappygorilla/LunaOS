Welcome to LunaOS

Your desktop. Your rules. LunaOS is an experimental Arch Linux desktop built around KDE Plasma.

Start here

  1. Connect to a network from the system tray.
  2. Open LunaOS Control Center and choose a profile.
  3. Review switches, then apply the profile.
  4. Test hardware before installation.
Experimental image: Installation may erase the disk you choose. The installer is guided Archinstall. Back up data and review all disk selections carefully.
